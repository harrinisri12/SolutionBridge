Challenge Overview

Sanction ID: MH-PUN-WTR-2026

Problem Statement

The municipal water network experiences significant non-revenue water (NRW) losses caused by undetected subterranean pipeline rupture, localized pressure surges, delayed fault localization, and non-instrumented legacy distribution trunks spanning over 420 kilometers of dense metropolitan grid.

Expected Procurement Outcome

Deploy a robust, interoperable edge-computed leakage detection solution capable of isolating and geo-locating abnormal water loss events in real-world municipal water pipeline circuits with metric precision, sub-half-day escalation alert cycles, and bidirectional telemetry handshake into the Municipal Smart City Integrated Command & Control Centre (ICCC).

Target Water Loss Reduction ≥ 20% Benchmark against Ward 14 baseline
Detection Accuracy ≥ 90% Acoustic & high-precision transient nodes
Mean Incident Triage < 12 Hours Anomaly detection to field repair order
