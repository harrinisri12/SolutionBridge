# SolutionBridge — From Problems to Proven Solution

SolutionBridge is a comprehensive National Innovation Procurement Platform connecting Government Challenges with Innovative Startup Solutions across the complete lifecycle:

1. **Problem Definition**: Government departments identify pressing public sector challenges.
2. **Startup Discovery & Proposals**: Startups submit detailed technical solutions and milestones.
3. **Expert Evaluation**: Domain specialists review, score, and rank proposals with weighted scorecards.
4. **Pilot Deployment & Real-time Logs**: Sandboxed trials with real-time KPI metrics and telemetry.
5. **Independent Third-Party Audits**: Verification of baseline vs actual outcomes with cryptographic audit seals.
6. **Scale-up Procurement**: Direct procurement qualification and automated milestone disbursement.
